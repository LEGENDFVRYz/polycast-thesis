Bridging conventional whiteboard teaching with structured digital archiving

ABOUT US
PolyCast: Assistive Instructor Marker Device for Classroom Content Capture is an academic research initiative focused on supporting traditional classroom instruction through digital whiteboard content capture and preservation.
TECHNOLOGY
The system integrates motion tracking, spatial positioning, and writing-state detection technologies—utilizing advanced sensor fusion—into a marker-based device capable of converting handwritten board content into structured digital notes.
By automatically preserving instructional writing, PolyCast is designed to assist classroom note documentation and provide an additional reference for students, particularly in computation-heavy subjects such as Mathematics and Engineering, and other technical disciplines.
OBJECTIVE
Traditional face-to-face instruction often requires students to divide their attention between understanding complex concepts and hastily copying notes from the whiteboard.
PolyCast seeks to bridge conventional whiteboard teaching with structured digital archiving. The goal is to maintain the natural, unhindered flow of face-to-face instruction while ensuring that no critical computations or diagrams are lost when the board is erased.
How the System Works
PolyCast pairs a custom hardware marker with a synchronized web platform to create a seamless archiving process:
Class
Administrator
An admin can be any designated representative of the class—such as a student president, the instructor, or an assigned facilitator.
They configure the digital workspace by setting up subject directories and specific session folders.

Once a live stream is initiated, the physical marker's movements are translated and saved directly into these folders as the lecture progresses. Admins retain full control to manage, delete, or recover these archives throughout the academic term.
Student
(Guest User)
Students simply visit the platform and wait for the class admin to initiate a session.

Once live, students can watch the whiteboard notes generate on their own screens. The platform allows students to freely navigate past notes in the gallery even while a stream is actively ongoing, and download complete note sequences with a single click.

The Research Team
PolyCast was developed by a team of Computer Engineering undergraduate researchers from Polytechnic University of the Philippines - Manila as part of their thesis initiative.
