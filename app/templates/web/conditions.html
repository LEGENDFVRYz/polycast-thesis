Description of Service
PolyCast provides an assistive digital archiving platform designed to capture, organize, and store whiteboard notes generated during face-to-face classroom instruction. The service relies on synchronized hardware (a sensor-equipped marker) and a web database to translate physical writing into downloadable digital formats.
User Roles and Responsibilities
The platform accommodates two distinct types of users, each with specific responsibilities:
  • Class Administrators (Account Security): Admins (instructors, class presidents, or designated representatives) are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their login credentials.
    • Content Management: Admins are solely responsible for configuring the digital workspace accurately (e.g., naming Subject and Session folders) and managing the generated archives.
    • Appropriate Use: Admins agree to initiate live streams only for educational, instructional, or relevant academic purposes.
  • Students or Guest Users (Access): Students are granted guest access to view live streams, browse the gallery, and download note sequences.
    • Appropriate Use: Guests agree to use the platform and the downloaded notes strictly for personal study and academic reference. Users must not attempt to disrupt the platform's functionality or access unauthorized administrative directories.

However, the actual academic content (the notes, diagrams, and lectures) captured during a session remains the intellectual property of the respective instructors or the academic institution. PolyCast simply acts as the medium for capturing and storing this instructional content.
