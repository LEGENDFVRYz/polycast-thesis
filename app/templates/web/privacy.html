How we collect, use, and protect your information.

This Privacy Policy outlines how the PolyCast research team ("we," "our," or "the system") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website and its associated hardware. As an academic thesis project developed at the Polytechnic University of the Philippines – Manila, we are committed to ensuring your privacy and operating with complete transparency.
Information We Collect
The PolyCast system collects only the minimum amount of data necessary to facilitate the digital archiving of classroom notes. The data collected depends entirely on how you interact with the platform:
Class Administrators (Registered Users): To configure subject directories and initiate live streams, designated class representatives or instructors must create an account. We collect basic login credentials, such as a username and password.
Students (Guest Users): The platform is designed for frictionless student access. We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) from students. Guest users are not required to create an account, provide an email, or submit any personal data to view the live streams, navigate the gallery, or download note sequences.
Hardware & System Data: The physical PolyCast marker transmits motion and spatial positioning data (via its IMU and UWB sensors) directly to the web platform to generate the digital notes. This is purely operational data used to reconstruct whiteboard writing and contains no personal information.
How We Use the Information
The information processed by the PolyCast platform is used strictly for operational and academic purposes:
  • To authenticate Class Administrators and grant them access to their designated subject and session folders in the database.
  • To allow Admins to manage, delete, and recover digital note archives.
  • To facilitate synchronization between the hardware marker device and the web application.
Data Storage and Security
All administrative credentials and generated digital notes are stored securely within our project database. Class Administrators maintain full control over the content generated during their sessions and have the active ability to delete or recover subject folders. Because this is an ongoing academic research project, data is retained only for the duration necessary to complete the testing, evaluation, and defense phases of the study.
Information Sharing and Disclosure
PolyCast is an independent academic initiative. We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer any user data or generated classroom notes to outside commercial parties. Access to the stored digital notes is restricted to the specific class environment managed by the logged-in Administrator and the guest students provided with the platform link.
